What counts

  • Public, on your own account, and about MausBot: the app, Cloud, or how you use them.
  • Honest. Say what you liked and what you did not. Nothing in the post is written by us.
  • Links to mausbot.com or the GitHub repository, so people can find what you are talking about.
  • Reach is what the platform shows when we review: views first, then likes, reposts and comments. Bought engagement does not count.
  • One reward per post. Post as often as you like, and send each one.
  • Where your platform asks for it, say the post may be rewarded. We will never ask you to hide that.

Questions

Which platforms count?
Any public one: X, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Reddit, Threads, a blog or a newsletter. If people can open it without an account, it counts.
How much will my post earn?
How much, and whether the Cloud month comes with it, is decided by the team when we review your post. Reach counts most, but a post that teaches something well or finds an audience we have not met can earn credits with modest numbers, and we often reshare the good ones.
How do I get the credits?
We email you at the address you give us, within a week of reviewing, to arrange the Claude API credits and switch on your Cloud month.
Can I send a post I made before this page existed?
Yes, as long as it is still public. Older posts are reviewed the same way.
Is this a paid review?
No. Nobody is paid before posting, and we never ask for a particular opinion. Rewards go to posts that have already reached people, whatever they concluded.
Who sees what I send?
Your link, name, email and note go to a private Discord channel that only the two of us who run MausBot can read. We use the email only to reply to you.
