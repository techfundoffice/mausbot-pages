How it works

Three steps, one account.

  1. 1

    Pick a plan

    Choose Starter, Pro or Max and subscribe through a secure hosted checkout. We then email you a link that signs you in to your Cloud.

  2. 2

    Open in the app

    Choose Open in the app on your Cloud page, or go to Settings → MausBot Cloud in the desktop app and sign in. The app connects to your cloud workspace.

  3. 3

    Sign in to your AI

    Sign in to Claude or ChatGPT on your Cloud, or add an API key. Your bots are ready to work around the clock.

A few things to know

Can I subscribe now?

Yes. Each plan's Get button takes you to a secure hosted checkout, billed monthly, and we email you a link that signs you in to your Cloud. You can cancel any time from the billing page.

Which plan should I pick?

Every plan runs your bots on a cloud workspace of their own that stays on around the clock, with cloud computers by Boat, voice and automations. Starter is the smallest and lowest-priced. The plans differ in how many computer-hours and how much speech you get a month, how big the cloud machine is, and support: Pro and Max get priority email support.

Does Cloud include AI usage?

No, on any plan. Connect your own Claude or ChatGPT account, or an API key, on your Cloud machine. Heavy 24/7 use can hit your plan’s limits; Claude Max or an API key is recommended.

What can automations do?

Your bots act on a schedule, when another app sends a webhook, or when something happens in a connected app, such as a new Gmail email, a HubSpot deal or a Stripe payment. Gmail is checked every 15 minutes; other apps arrive within seconds. Tell a bot in a sentence, such as “when I get an email from Acme, draft a reply”, and it sets one up. On Cloud they run with your laptop closed.

Is the cloud desktop included?

Yes, on every plan. Your bots get cloud computers from our partner Boat (boat.dev): as many as they need, up to 15 at once, each with a live desktop you can watch or take over, with no account or key to set up. How many computer-hours you get a month depends on your plan.

Can I call my bots?

Yes, on every plan, and in the free app too. Live voice calls use your own OpenAI key, and OpenAI bills each call to it: add the key in Settings → API keys. The voice included with Cloud is for your bots’ spoken replies.

Are there limits?

Cloud computers and voice are covered by fair use. Cloud computers: up to 15 at once, on every plan. Computer-hours and speech are set by plan. Starter: 100 computer-hours a month and about 30 minutes of speech a month; Pro: 300 computer-hours a month and about an hour of speech a month; Max: 800 computer-hours a month and about 3 hours of speech a month. A small computer counts as half an hour, and allowances reset on the 1st of each month (UTC). AI model usage is set by your own provider’s plan.

How does support work?

Email milind.soni@supamaus.com or omkar@supamaus.com. Every plan gets email support; messages from Pro and Max subscribers are answered first.

Do I need Cloud to use MausBot?

No. The free, open-source app keeps working locally without a Cloud account. Cloud is an optional service, not a replacement for the app.
