The service

MausBot Cloud gives you a personal cloud machine that stays on, so your bots can keep working while your own computer is off. You manage it from the MausBot app and your Cloud account.

You can cancel at any time in Cloud. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you have paid for, and then does not renew. Payments are not refunded for part of a month, except as our refund policy describes.
