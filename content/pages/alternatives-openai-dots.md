OpenAI dots alternative: MausBot

An OpenAI dots alternative with no Pro plan and no region lock: a free, open source team of agents on your own machine, on the ChatGPT account you have.

OpenAI dots are always-on agents inside ChatGPT, one dot per person, GPT only, for Pro and Business Premium subscribers, and not on Pro in the EEA, Switzerland or the UK. MausBot is the open source dots alternative: a team of agents on your own machine, on any model, free, with no plan or region requirement.

What OpenAI dots is

Dots launched at DevDay on September 29, 2026. A dot lives inside ChatGPT: you name it, it gets a handle and an avatar, and it works from its own OpenAI-hosted cloud computer on GPT-6 Astra. It reaches 4,000+ apps through ChatGPT plugins, runs scheduled tasks and read-only background research, and answers you in ChatGPT, Slack and Teams. One primary dot today; OpenAI says it envisions teams of dots over time.

Launched: September 29, 2026 . Sources: OpenAI: Introducing dots ; OpenAI help: getting started with your dot .

Why people look for a OpenAI dots alternative

Only on ChatGPT Pro, reported from $100 a month, and Business Premium; Free, Go and Plus get nothing.

Pro excludes the EEA, Switzerland and the UK, and OpenAI has said a launch there is not planned for now.

One dot, one model: no Claude or Grok, and no second agent yet.

Memory you cannot edit: OpenAI's FAQ says you cannot view, delete or modify individual dot memories; the only way to clear them is to delete the dot.

Training on personal plans follows your model-improvement setting and can include the dot's actions and connected-app data.

What MausBot is

MausBot is a free, open-source chat app where every contact is an AI bot with its own model, its own computer, and its own connected apps. Bots run on the claude , codex , and grok CLIs installed on your own machine, with your existing logins, so one bot can be on Claude and the next on ChatGPT. Shell commands and file edits surface as Allow / Deny cards before they run, bots share group rooms and hand work to each other, and your data stays in ~/.openmausbot on your disk. It is published on GitHub under Apache 2.0, with iOS and Android companions, and paid Cloud plans from $15 a month keep bots working with the laptop closed.

What OpenAI dots still does better

4,000+ apps through ChatGPT plugins, about eight times what MausBot connects to.

Shares memory with ChatGPT and app permissions with ChatGPT Work and Codex.

Answers you in Slack and Teams, and keeps working with your laptop closed, with nothing to set up.
