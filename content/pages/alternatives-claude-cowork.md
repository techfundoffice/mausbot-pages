Claude Cowork alternative: MausBot

A Claude Cowork alternative that is open source and runs Claude beside Codex and Grok: a team of agents on your own machine, free, on the Claude plan you have.

Claude Cowork is Anthropic's app for giving Claude your files, apps and browser, now part of the single Claude experience on every paid plan. MausBot is the open source Cowork alternative: a team of agents on your own machine where a Claude bot sits next to Codex and Grok bots, with approval cards and your data on your own disk.

What Claude Cowork is

Cowork launched as a macOS research preview on January 12, 2026, reached Windows on February 10, went GA in April, and on September 16, 2026 was folded into one unified Claude experience where Claude picks the mode. It plans, spawns sub-agents, reads and writes files, produces Office documents, researches the web and runs in a sandboxed Linux VM, with the desktop kept open for local files and the browser, with connectors, scheduled tasks and cloud sessions that continue with the laptop closed.

Launched: January 12, 2026 (preview); merged into Claude on September 16, 2026 . Sources: Anthropic support: get started with Claude Cowork .

Why people look for a Claude Cowork alternative

Single vendor, single model: no Codex or Grok, no local models, no bring-your-own key.

Closed source: no harness to read, nothing to audit or air-gap.

Usage caps: user reports of Pro subscribers hitting limits in minutes on agentic tasks.

The desktop must stay open for local file, browser and computer access.

What MausBot is

MausBot is a free, open-source chat app where every contact is an AI bot with its own model, its own computer, and its own connected apps. Bots run on the claude , codex , and grok CLIs installed on your own machine, with your existing logins, so one bot can be on Claude and the next on ChatGPT. Shell commands and file edits surface as Allow / Deny cards before they run, bots share group rooms and hand work to each other, and your data stays in ~/.openmausbot on your disk. It is published on GitHub under Apache 2.0, with iOS and Android companions, and paid Cloud plans from $15 a month keep bots working with the laptop closed.

What Claude Cowork still does better

Produces Excel, PowerPoint, Word and PDF documents directly.

A Chrome side panel, cloud sessions that continue with the laptop closed, and first-party connectors to Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace.

Made by the company that makes the model.
