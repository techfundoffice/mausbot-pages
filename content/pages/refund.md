Refund Policy

Last updated 10 October 2026

The short version: you can cancel any time, and your plan runs to the end of the month you have paid for.

Cancel any time

You can cancel at any time in your Cloud account. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you have already paid for, and then it is not renewed.

Free trials

If you started a free trial while Cloud offered one, nothing is charged during it. Cancel before it ends and you pay nothing. If you do not cancel, your first payment is taken when the trial ends.

No refunds for part of a month

Payments are not refunded for the part of a month you do not use, including when you cancel partway through it.

When we refund

We refund a payment that should not have been taken: if you were charged twice for the same month, or charged after you cancelled. We also refund wherever the law requires it.

If we stop offering MausBot Cloud for reasons that are not about you, we refund the time you have paid for and not used, as our terms say.

How to ask for a refund

Email milind.soni@supamaus.com from the address on your Cloud account, with the date of the payment or the order number on your receipt, and tell us what went wrong. We will confirm by email when the refund is issued.

How refunds are paid

Refunds are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record, back to the payment method you used. Your bank or card issuer may take several business days to show it.

Your rights

This policy does not affect any rights you have under the consumer law where you live.
