Find the right candidates.

Agents read each requirement, search your ATS and candidate database, and rank matches on skills, rate, location, and availability, with the reason for each match.

Screen by phone or text.

Agents call or message shortlisted candidates, ask the questions you set, and log every answer in your ATS. Anyone who’d rather talk to a person goes to a recruiter.

Approve with one tap.

Agents draft each submission from the résumé and screen notes. A recruiter approves, edits, or rejects it before it reaches the client or hiring manager.

Before you deploy

Does it work with our ATS? +

That depends on your ATS. Many have an API that agents can use to read roles and update candidates; others can be worked through email or the browser. We’ll check yours and confirm the approach before setup.

Do candidates know they’re talking to an AI? +

Agents introduce themselves the way you set. We recommend saying at the start that it’s an AI agent and offering a recruiter to anyone who prefers one. Rules on automated calls and AI in hiring vary by location, so check the ones that apply to you.

Who makes the hiring decision? +

Your team. Agents gather information, screen against the criteria you set, and draft submissions. A recruiter approves every submission, and hiring decisions stay with people.

What does an AI hiring workflow cost? +

The MausBot app is free and open source. A hiring deployment also needs model usage, hosting, phone service for screening calls, and integration work. Pricing depends on your volume and setup; book a call to discuss the initial and ongoing costs.
