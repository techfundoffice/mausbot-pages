Before you deploy

Can we use MausBot with our existing phone number? +

That depends on your phone provider and call routing. We’ll check whether your existing number can connect or forward calls to the agents, and confirm any changes before setup.

What happens when an agent can’t help? +

You set the escalation rules: when to transfer a call, which team receives it, and what to do if nobody is available. We’ll agree on the information passed along so your team knows what has already happened.

What does an AI call center cost? +

The MausBot app is free and open source. A call-center deployment also needs phone service, model usage, hosting, and integration work. Pricing depends on your call volume and setup; book a call to discuss the initial and ongoing costs.
