Enter it once. Spot problems early.

Enter records once.

Agents read delivery notes, supplier bills, and production logs, including photos of handwritten sheets, and enter them in your ERP or spreadsheets. Anything unclear goes to a person.

Raise and chase invoices.

Agents prepare invoices from dispatch records, match supplier bills to purchase orders and goods received, and send payment reminders on the schedule you set.

Keep orders on track.

Agents follow each order from purchase to dispatch, keep stock counts current, and answer “where is it?” from customers and your team. They flag parts that fall below reorder levels.

Watch the machines.

Agents check frames from your CCTV cameras at the interval you set. When a machine stops, a warning light comes on, or an aisle is blocked, they alert the right supervisor with the frame.

Before you deploy

Which cameras and CCTV systems work? +

Most IP cameras and network video recorders (NVRs) can share still frames or a stream on your local network, and agents check those frames at the interval you set. We’ll check your camera system and network, and agree which cameras and areas to include, before setup.

Does it work with our ERP or accounting software? +

That depends on the software. Agents can work through an API where one exists, through imports and exports, or through the software’s own screens on a computer you provide. We’ll check yours and confirm the approach before setup.

Does our data leave the plant? +

The MausBot app runs on a computer you own, so it can reach the cameras and systems on your local network. The AI models agents use may run in the cloud, so we’ll agree which frames and records they can send before setup.

What does it cost? +

The MausBot app is free and open source. A manufacturing deployment also needs model usage, hosting, and integration work for your systems and cameras. Pricing depends on your volume and setup; book a call to discuss the initial and ongoing costs.
