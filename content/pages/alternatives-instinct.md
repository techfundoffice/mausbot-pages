Instinct alternative: MausBot

An Instinct AI alternative you can download today: open source, signs into your apps over OAuth instead of holding passwords, and asks before it acts.

Instinct is an invite-only personal agent you text on iMessage or WhatsApp; it runs on a cloud computer that holds your passwords and acts without asking by default. MausBot is the open source Instinct alternative: a team of agents on your own machine that sign in over OAuth, ask before they act, and need no invite.

What Instinct is

Instinct is the personal agent from Spear Street Technology, founded by Noah Shinn. There is no app: you text it on iMessage or WhatsApp, or call it, and it works from a persistent cloud computer with your logins cached. It went viral in late August 2026 and raised $350 million at a $2.5 billion valuation by August 26. Invites were paused in mid-September while the company scales compute.

Launched: Viral launch, late August 2026 (invite-only beta) . Sources: TechCrunch: Instinct's assistant raises privacy and security concerns ; Instinct .

What MausBot is

MausBot is a free, open-source chat app where every contact is an AI bot with its own model, its own computer, and its own connected apps. Bots run on the claude , codex , and grok CLIs installed on your own machine, with your existing logins, so one bot can be on Claude and the next on ChatGPT. Shell commands and file edits surface as Allow / Deny cards before they run, bots share group rooms and hand work to each other, and your data stays in ~/.openmausbot on your disk. It is published on GitHub under Apache 2.0, with iOS and Android companions, and paid Cloud plans from $15 a month keep bots working with the laptop closed.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an Instinct invite? Through the waitlist on instinct.com or from an existing member. As of mid-September 2026, invites are paused. MausBot has no invite. Is Instinct safe to use? That depends on your tolerance: its terms grant a perpetual licence over what you share, and early users reported an unapproved email and a successful prompt injection. MausBot's answer is structural: the agent runs on your machine, signs in over OAuth, and asks before it acts. Does MausBot need my passwords? No. Apps connect over OAuth through Composio, so a bot gets a scoped token you can revoke, never your password. Can MausBot text me on iMessage? No. MausBot is its own chat app, on desktop with iOS and Android companions.
