Who it's for

Every bot needs a model to think with, a computer to work on and tools to reach. If you sell one of them, our users are looking for you.

Models and inference

Inference providers and model platforms. Users add your API key and pick your models for their bots.

Computers

Virtual computers, sandboxes and browsers for agents. Users connect your account and bots do their work on your machines.

Tools and apps

Agent tools and API integrations, from social schedulers like Postiz to search, email and data. Users connect your service once and the whole team uses it.

How it works

Book a 30-minute call. We show you where your product would appear and agree the details.

Send us your details

We email you for your logo, links, API docs and a test key, and build or check the integration.

Go live

Your spot appears in the app and on the site, and we post the launch video.

Questions

Do you resell our API? No. Users bring their own API key, so they sign up with you, pay you, and stay your customers. Does being a partner change what users get by default? No. Partners are featured and labelled as partners, but users still choose their own model, computer and tools. That keeps the placement trustworthy, which is what makes it worth having. What if we don't have an integration yet? We build it. Send us your API docs and a test key, and we'll connect your product to the app before your spot goes live. How many partners do you take? A few in each category, so every spot stays visible. If your category is full, we'll tell you before you pay.
