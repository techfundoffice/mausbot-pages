Plug in. Pair. Talk.

No setup weekend, no terminal. Three steps and your AI lives with you.

  1. Step 1

    Plug it in

    Power and an Ethernet cable into the bottom layer, or your Wi-Fi. MausBox starts its models by itself.

  2. Step 2

    Pair your apps

    The front display shows a six-digit code. Type it into MausBot on your computer or phone.

  3. Step 3

    Start talking

    Ask from anywhere you use MausBot. The thinking happens on the box, at home.

Tech specs

A concept. Final specs, price and availability may change.

Chip
AMD Ryzen AI Max+ 395: 16 Zen 5 cores, Radeon 8060S graphics, an XDNA 2 NPU with 50 TOPS
Memory
128 GB LPDDR5X-8000, shared by the CPU, the graphics and the NPU
Local AI
Open models up to 120B parameters run on the box, and keep working offline
Storage
2 TB PCIe 4.0 SSD in the top layer, with a second M.2 slot
Expansion
Stack another storage layer on top for more drives
Network
5 Gb Ethernet and Wi-Fi 7
Ports
Two USB4 (40 Gb/s), two USB-A (10 Gb/s), HDMI 2.1, and USB-C on the front
Power
240 W supply, built in. No battery: it stays plugged in and on
Size
About 156 × 156 × 88 mm, around 2 litres
Finish
Machined aluminium layers, hexagon vents and green light rings
Software
MausBot, ready at first boot, with updates you approve
