Safe to build on

The core is Apache 2.0 licensed and always will be. The business features sit on top of it, in the same app.

Open-source core

The app is public on GitHub. Your security team can read every line before the first call.

One codebase

The same app people download from this site. A licence key switches on the business features — there is no separate fork to fall behind.

Your keys, your data

Bots run on your own model accounts, on your machines or your servers — unless you ask us to host them.

Nothing breaks at renewal

If a licence lapses, the app falls back to the open-source edition. Your bots and your data stay exactly where they are.
